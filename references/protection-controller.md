# Protection controller

`python scripts/live.py protection-preview --workdir trader` reads current quotes and intraday account books and saves a local decision. `protection-step` uses the existing two-stage human confirmation for one proposed action, refreshing observations before submission. No background service or unattended submission is enabled by these commands.

Add `protection-policy.json` to the ordinary live work directory. Required fields are `schema: protection_policy_v1`, a unique string `policy_id`, exact `broker`, `account_id`, `exchange`, `symbol`, `token`, `product: MIS`, current IST `date`, `entry_side` (BUY for a long position or SELL for a short), whole-lot `max_quantity`, positive on-tick `stop_trigger`, `stop_limit`, `target`, `exit_limit`, and `flat_by` matching config. The quantity cap must not exceed the current freeze limit. For longs, stop limit must be at or below the trigger and target above it; reverse the inequalities for shorts. This is an explicit user policy, not a recommended trading strategy.

The controller retains an exact waiting stop; cancels conflicting orders before replacement; and latches an exit when the stop, target or inclusive cutoff is reached. The exit decision persists in `state/protection.sqlite` across cancellation delays, restarts and subsequent price reversals. It recomputes remaining units after every fill or terminal cancellation. A valid pending limit exit is observed without duplicate submission. An explicit limit may remain unfilled after a gap; no market conversion or automatic repricing occurs.

Unknown order states, unresolved execution intents, stale quotes, inconsistent positions, carry products, direction reversals and quantities above the policy cap block. The shared `state/execution.sqlite` retains submission ambiguity. Never delete it to clear a lock. After a flat observation without working selected-instrument orders, the policy completes; new exposure requires a new reviewed policy ID. Changing a policy under the same ID blocks. Flatness applies to the selected instrument at that observation, not the entire account.

New stop/exit placements require dated instrument-specific session evidence. An unavailable API, closed session or rejected order can prevent protection or flattening; use broker controls and reconcile afterwards. Local state is not an exchange OCO order. See [execution](execution.md) and [session evidence](india-expansion.md).

Offline tests cover five broker payloads, long/short positions, partial entries, cancellation/fill races, persistent exit decisions, completion and a simulated human-operated CLI step. They do not establish authenticated broker or production acceptance. Official contracts checked on 11 November 2026: [Kite orders](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/orders/) and [NSE market timings](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/market-timings).
