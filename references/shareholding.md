# Shareholding pattern and promoter pledge

`scripts/shareholding.py` answers "who owns this company, how did that change last quarter, and are promoter shares pledged?" from NSE's own filings. Research only; nothing here is a recommendation.

## Commands

```text
python scripts/shareholding.py ADANIPORTS
python scripts/shareholding.py JSWSTEEL --summary
python scripts/shareholding.py ADANIPORTS --format json --out reports/adaniports-shp.json
```

Use the NSE symbol (not a BSE code). `--summary` prints a short answer-first digest; `--format json` returns schema `shareholding_v1`.

## What it shows

- **Quarter and filing date.** The latest quarter-end shareholding pattern (31 Mar, 30 Jun, 30 Sep, 31 Dec) and the date NSE broadcast it; a revised filing replaces the original. Ad-hoc filings (for example before a share issue) are skipped.
- **Holders, with the change from the previous quarter in percentage points:** promoters and promoter group; foreign institutions (the exchange's "Institutions (Foreign)" category: FPIs, plus foreign direct investment where a foreign partner holds shares, shown separately); domestic institutions, with mutual funds and insurance companies; government (public category, shown only when non-zero); retail and others (the "Non-institutions" category, with individuals holding up to ₹2 lakh); employee trusts; total public. These match Screener.in's Promoters / FIIs / DIIs / Government / Public lines (checked on ADANIPORTS, September 2026 quarter, to 0.01 point).
- **Number of shareholders** this quarter and last.
- **Promoter pledge and encumbrance.** From the filing: shares pledged as a percentage of the promoters' own holding and of all shares, and shares otherwise encumbered (non-disposal undertakings and other encumbrances). From NSE's promoter-pledge disclosure (SEBI SAST Regulation 31 data, updated between filings): the same two percentages and the date NSE last updated them. The headline uses the filing; the disclosure is the cross-check.
- **Public shareholders above 1%**, named in the same XBRL (SEBI requires every public holder above 1% to be named): name, type (insurer, mutual fund, foreign portfolio investor, company and so on) and the change from the previous quarter, or "new above 1%". Promoter-group entities and "category" rows are left out. For ADANIPORTS in September 2026: LIC 6.43%, GQG Partners International Opportunities Fund 1.72%, SBI Nifty 50 ETF 1.45%, New World Fund 1.28%.
- **Promoter holding by quarter** for up to eight quarters, from NSE's filing list, with its shape: first and latest, peak, low, and the largest quarterly rise and the largest quarterly fall, each with its quarter. First-to-last alone hides the path (ADANIPORTS: 65.89% in December 2024 and 66.03% in September 2026, but 68.02% in between: +2.13 points in the December 2025 quarter and −1.99 in the June 2026 quarter).
- **Large promoter moves.** A quarter in which the promoter holding moved more than 1 point gets a pointer line, matched against NSE's SAST Regulation 29 disclosures (one request, made only when such a move exists): promoter-group filings whose transaction period ends in that quarter (or, without a period, filed in it or within ten days after) are named with the share moved, how it happened and the filing date. NSE's acquisition-mode field is not taken as fact: when the company's total share count (from the two quarters' shareholding XBRL) rose by about the stake acquired, the line says the shares were newly allotted; otherwise it quotes the field as "mode as filed". ADANIPORTS: "Promoter stake +2.13 pts in the Dec 2025 quarter: NSE SAST (Reg. 29) filings by the promoter group in that window: Carmichael Rail and Port Singapore Holding PVT LTD acquired 6.24% (allotment of new shares: share count 216.01 cr → 230.40 cr, +14,38,20,153, likely a preferential issue; filed 29 Dec 2025); Emerging Market Investment DMCC acquired 0.67% (mode as filed: open market; filed 29 Dec 2025)". NSE's row says "Open Market" for the first, but it was the preferential allotment of 14,38,20,153 shares for NQXT. The line also names promoter entities whose holding changed by at least 0.25 point between the two filings and that no SAST filing names ("Worldwide Emerging Market Holding Limited 3.74% → 1.75%" in the June 2026 quarter). With no matching SAST filing, NSE's bulk- and block-deal history for the symbol and quarter is read (two requests, only then) and deals by promoter entities are named with the buyers or sellers on the other side at the same price; if there are none the line says so (an off-market transfer or a trade reported only on BSE does not appear there: ADANIPORTS' June 2026 fall shows no NSE bulk or block deal), and if the lists cannot be loaded it keeps the pointer to NSE's SAST, preferential-allotment and block-deal filings. SAST filings cover acquisitions crossing 5% and later 2% moves, so a preferential allotment or a block deal below those levels will not appear there.
- **Pledge trend.** The pledged (and all encumbered) share of the promoter holding in each quarter of the promoter trend (up to eight), read from each quarter's shareholding XBRL in parallel (filings before mid-2025 give one "pledged or encumbered" figure). ADANIPORTS: "Promoter pledge over Dec 2024–Sep 2026 (8 quarterly filings): none in any quarter, and nothing otherwise encumbered." The full report lists each quarter.
- **SAST threshold and BSE.** When a large promoter move has no SAST filing and its net change is below 2% of the shares, the line says none was required (SAST Reg. 29(2) asks a 5%+ promoter group to disclose each 2% move, counted from its last disclosure, so smaller moves can add up). When NSE's bulk and block-deal lists show no promoter deal, it says the trade may have been on BSE and gives BSE's bulk- and block-deal pages (https://www.bseindia.com/markets/equity/EQReports/tra_Bulk_Deals.aspx and tra_Block_Deals.aspx), which block scripted access, so open them in a browser or search the web.
- **Fund houses across quarters.** Each filing names a mutual fund house's or LIC's whole holding by one of its schemes, and the scheme changes from quarter to quarter (Bharti Airtel: LIC's 4.14% was "LICI New Pension Plus Secured Fund" in June 2026 and its 4.46% "LICI Index Plus Flexi Smart Growth Fund" in September; SBI, ICICI Prudential and HDFC the same). Mutual funds, insurers and NPS Trust accounts are therefore matched by fund house (LIC, SBI, ICICI Prudential, HDFC, Nippon India, Kotak, Axis, UTI, Mirae Asset, Aditya Birla Sun Life, DSP and others; NPS accounts by pension-fund manager), shown as "LIC (all schemes)" or "SBI Mutual Fund (all schemes)" with the scheme named in the full table, and never called "new above 1%" when the same house was named last quarter. Other holders match by entity name ("Government of Singapore - E" is "Government of Singapore"). Holders named last quarter but not now are listed as "no longer named above 1%".
- **Older formats.** Filings of early and mid 2025 give percentages as percentages (52.42) rather than fractions; they are scaled. Filings before the 2025 format do not mark which named holders are promoters: the promoter table's rows (individuals/HUF and "any other", Indian and foreign) are taken as the promoter entities when they add up to the promoter category within half a point, and are never listed as public holders; otherwise that filing names no promoter entities, and a promoter move compared with it says so instead of calling entities "not listed".
- **Government.** The public category's Central / State Government line (the President of India in Vodafone Idea, 49%) is shown apart from domestic institutions, with Central Government, state governments and government-controlled companies under it when there is more than one; a government promoter (Coal India) shows as "of which Central / State Government (President of India)" under promoters.
- **Promoter entities.** The summary names every promoter entity with its holding (the eight largest, then the rest counted and added up). A note beside an entity comes only from the filing (a short sentence in its notes that starts with the entity's name, and its significant beneficial owners) or from the curated list of well-known holding vehicles in `assets/business_peers.json` ("promoter_vehicles", each with its source and check date, marked "[curated list]"); nothing is inferred from a name.
- **Summary** (`--summary`): promoter holding and pledge, the other categories with domestic institutions split into mutual funds and insurers, the trend shape, one pointer line for large promoter moves and the named holders above 1%. Counts use Indian digit grouping (shareholders 10,70,249).

- **Pledge or something else.** A formal pledge (lenders can sell the shares on a default) is reported apart from non-disposal undertakings
  (NDUs: a promise to lenders not to sell or pledge) and other encumbrance, naming what the filing ticks. Quarters filed in the older format
  (before mid-2025) give pledges and other encumbrance as one figure: their pledge is "no separate pledge figure" (no data), never "none",
  unless nothing at all was encumbered that quarter.
- **Encumbrance filings by lenders and their trustees.** NSE exposes no separate SAST Regulation 31 list, but each lender or security trustee that
  takes or releases security over 5% or more of the shares files a SAST disclosure; those inside the trend window are summarised (created,
  released or invoked, with the date, the holder and the share covered). Vedanta, 2026: GLAS Agency, Citicorp, Axis Trustee and Kroll as
  trustees for the promoter's lenders. One request, made only when the trend shows encumbrance or a large promoter move.
- **No promoter group.** A professionally managed company (Eternal, HDFC Bank, ITC) files no promoter category; the report says so instead of
  failing or printing a pledge gap.

## Sources

| Data | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Filing list (quarters, promoter/public totals, XBRL link) | `https://www.nseindia.in/api/corporate-share-holdings-master?index=equities&symbol=SYMBOL` |
| Category holdings, pledged and encumbered shares | the company's shareholding XBRL on `nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/xbrl/SHP_*.xml` |
| Promoter-pledge disclosure | `https://www.nseindia.in/api/corporate-pledgedata?index=equities&symbol=SYMBOL` |
| SAST Regulation 29 disclosures (large promoter moves, or any promoter encumbrance in the trend) | `https://www.nseindia.in/api/corporate-sast-reg29?index=equities&symbol=SYMBOL` |
| Bulk and block deals (a large promoter move with no SAST filing only) | `https://www.nseindia.in/api/historicalOR/bulk-block-short-deals?optionType=bulk_deals\|block_deals&symbol=SYMBOL&from=DD-MM-YYYY&to=DD-MM-YYYY` |

Requests go through an allowlist (`shareholding.nse_request`): only these NSE endpoints with an exact `index=equities&symbol=` query, the bulk/block-deal history with exactly `optionType` (bulk_deals or block_deals), `symbol`, `from` and `to` (at most a year apart), NSE's results, annual-results (`corporates-financial-results`, with the fixed `period=Annual`) and corporate actions endpoints used by stock analysis, and shareholding (`SHP_*`) and results XBRL files under `/corporate/xbrl/` on NSE's archive (results for companies, NBFCs, banks and insurers: `INTEGRATED_FILING_INDAS_*`, `_NBFC_INDAS_*`, `_BANKING_*`, `_LI_*`, `_GI_*`, and the older annual files without the `INTEGRATED_FILING_` prefix such as `INDAS_*`). No redirects are followed, responses are size-capped, and XML with a DOCTYPE or entity declaration is refused. The endpoints answer plain requests without a login or cookie (checked 18 Nov 2026). No aggregator is used.

## Limits and failure behaviour

- A shareholding pattern is filed within 21 days of the quarter end, so it can be about four months old; filings older than about 140 days are flagged.
- If a quarter's XBRL cannot be read, the report falls back to the promoter and public totals in NSE's filing list and lists the gap. If the pledge disclosure is unavailable, the filing's own pledge figures still stand and the gap is listed. If NSE lists no quarterly filings, the run stops with a clear message.
- Category names follow the 2022 SEBI format. Older filings with a single "Institutions" block are split into domestic and foreign using the FPI lines.
- NSE's pledge disclosure also reports shares pledged in the depository system by all holders (margin pledges by brokers' clients and others). That figure is kept in the JSON (`depository_pledged_pct_all_holders`) but is not promoter pledge and is not shown in the report.

## Related: exchange disclosures feed

`scripts/research.py disclosures --symbols SYMBOL.NS` lists insider, takeover (SAST), pledge, bulk and block items from NSE's public RSS feeds. Those feeds hold only about a day of items and most announcement items carry no link, so an empty list does not mean there were no filings: the output says `"clearance": "none"` with a plain `clearance_note`. (`no_event_clearance: true` is kept for older readers; it means "this report never clears events", not "no events".) For pledges and shareholding use `shareholding.py`.
